Our Story

Originally from New Zealand, Sam moved to Australia straight out of school, where he quickly caught the wine bug while working in restaurants. Immersed in retail and sommelier roles, he got to experience wine from all corners of the world, meet inspiring winemakers, and explore countless cellars. In 2015, he completed his first harvest. Over the following years, he travelled to work harvests for a diverse range of wineries, learning the nuances of both large and small-scale production.

Driven to master grape growing, Sam returned to New Zealand in 2018 to work directly in the vineyards and winery. Then, in 2022, Sam and his young family relocated to Catalonia. He instantly fell in love with the region's ancient varieties, deep history, and unique terroir—particularly the expressive Trepat grape. Today, Sam crafts wines and works the soils of Solivella, a village nestled on the western edge of the basin within the DO Conca de Barberà.
